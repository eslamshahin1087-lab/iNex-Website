KNOWLEDGE & AWARENESS

Ask. Understand.
Choose with confidence.

Insurance awareness helps you look beyond a policy title: which benefits are included, what limits apply, which exclusions and deductibles matter, and what procedures you should know before a claim or renewal. Understanding these details earlier leads to better questions and a more informed review of options.

ESSENTIAL TOPICS

Knowledge to help you
ask better questions.

These topics build practical knowledge before purchasing, renewing or using insurance. They are a starting point for learning and asking better questions—not a substitute for reading your policy, consulting a qualified professional or relying on the final terms issued by the insurer.

01 / POLICY BASICS
▤

How do you read an insurance policy?

Review who is insured and which people or property are covered, the policy period, sums insured and coverage limits. Then check the benefits schedule, exclusions, deductibles, and notification, claims and renewal conditions.

02 / COVERAGE
◇

What does your policy cover?

Distinguish the insured risk from the benefit provided, the maximum indemnity and any deductible, and check the eligibility conditions for each benefit.

03 / EXCLUSIONS
!

Understand exclusions and conditions

Exclusions and special conditions define the actual scope of protection. Review what is not covered, any waiting periods, limitations and required procedures.

04 / RENEWAL
↻

Renew without surprises

Review changes in your needs, details, asset values or beneficiaries, and check coverage limits, terms, price and renewal date before the policy expires.

05 / COMPARISON
⇄

Compare coverage, not just price

Compare like for like: coverage type and limits, exclusions and deductibles, provider networks where relevant, and claims procedures. Ask what differs in terms and services, then weigh price against how well the offer fits your needs.

06 / CLAIMS
✓

Be prepared to make a claim

Check the claims section for the required contact, deadline and channel, and prepare the documents requested by your policy or insurer. Keep copies of submissions, reference numbers and correspondence. Submitting a claim does not guarantee approval; it is assessed under the policy terms.

07 / COMPLAINTS
?

If you need to make a complaint

Document the issue and contact the insurer through its official channel, keeping the complaint reference and correspondence. If it remains unresolved, consult the Financial Regulatory Authority’s escalation route and current guidance.

Educational notice

General information is not insurance or legal advice and does not replace policy terms. Coverage and exclusions vary by product, insurer and final policy.

HOW KNOWLEDGE MAKES A DIFFERENCE

From your first question to
the moment you need cover.

Insurance awareness is an ongoing journey, not a step that ends when a policy is purchased. The earlier you understand your protection, the better equipped you are to define your needs, review your terms and follow the right procedures.

01Before choosing

Identify what you want to protect, understand your needs and prepare questions before requesting a quote.

02When reviewing a policy

Check benefits, limits, exclusions, deductibles and any waiting periods.

03While cover is active

Keep your information and documents organised, and track dates and changes that may affect your protection.

04At claim and renewal

Know notification channels and required documents, and review your needs before renewal.

PUT KNOWLEDGE INTO PRACTICE

Four moments worth
reviewing in every insurance journey.

The questions you need to ask change with each stage. Use this checklist to know what to review, then refer to your policy and current local guidance whenever needed.

01

Before requesting a quote

Define what you want to protect, prepare accurate information and ask about coverage types, limits and exclusions relevant to your needs.

02

Before a policy is issued

Check the insured parties, details, sums insured and policy period. Read the benefits schedule, conditions, exclusions and deductibles.

03

During the policy period

Keep the policy and correspondence, record reference numbers and report material changes through the channels and within the timeframes specified in the policy.

04

At claim or renewal time

Follow notification procedures, prepare required documents and review coverage and needs before renewal. Remember that claims are assessed under the policy terms.

READ THE DETAILS THAT MATTER

Three details that make a difference
when reading a policy.

Some insurance terms may sound similar, but they play different roles in defining coverage. Learn their general meaning, then check the exact wording in your policy because terms vary by product.

01◇

Coverage limits

Limits describe the maximum amount or financial scope that applies to a benefit or cover under the policy terms. Check whether sub-limits or special conditions also apply.

02!

Exclusions

Exclusions identify situations, risks or circumstances that are not covered. Reading them is just as important as reviewing the listed benefits.

03↻

Deductibles and waiting periods

Some benefits may involve a deductible or a specified waiting period under the policy. Check how it is calculated and when a benefit becomes available, where such terms apply.
