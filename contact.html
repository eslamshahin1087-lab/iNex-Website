FIND THE RIGHT ROUTE

To reach the right support,
start with the right channel.

The right contact depends on the nature of your enquiry. This guide helps you direct your question to the most relevant channel while protecting your personal information and documents.

01

iNex services

For account setup, awareness tools or digital-service questions, use the support channels shown inside the app.

02

Policy and coverage

For a policy clause, coverage limit, exclusion or claim follow-up, contact the insurer or broker named in your policy.

03

Consultation request

You can submit a consultation request from within iNex and describe the subject you need to discuss without sharing sensitive information through a public channel.

BEFORE YOU REACH OUT

A clearer enquiry starts
with the right information.

Describing the nature of your enquiry helps direct it to the right channel and reduces unnecessary back-and-forth. Do not send personal information or sensitive insurance documents through public channels.

01◎

Identify your question

Is your question about using iNex, understanding an insurance topic, a policy condition or a claim follow-up? Identifying the subject helps direct you more effectively.

02▤

Prepare your reference

When asking about a policy, have its reference number, insurer name and official contact details ready. Share only the information needed through the appropriate secure channel.

03⇄

Follow up with the right provider

Use iNex channels for application and available-service enquiries. Contact your broker or insurer to interpret policy terms or follow up on claims.
