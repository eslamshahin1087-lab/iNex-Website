CUSTOMER-CENTRED SOLUTIONS

Different needs.
Clearer protection choices.

The iNex experience starts with your real needs: what do you want to protect, what does your policy say about benefits and exclusions, and what information will help you discuss options with an insurance broker?

01 / INDIVIDUALS

For individuals

Understand your insurance needs, learn key concepts and review available policy information and requests in one place. Use the content and tools to prepare more specific questions before discussing your options.

  • Insurance awareness and tips
  • Personal protection assessment
  • Policy and renewal tracking
02 / FAMILIES

For families

Start by recognising how needs differ across family members, responsibilities and circumstances, then explore protection options to discuss with an insurance broker.

  • Understand family protection needs
  • Request an insurance consultation
  • Review insurance quotations
03 / EMPLOYEES

For employees

Learn about employee-related insurance coverage and use the customer tools available in iNex to understand what is recorded and what may need clarification.

  • Understand coverage and benefits
  • Refer to policies and information
  • Request support or guidance
04 / CORPORATE

For organisations

The iNex application includes a Corporate HR Hub for corporate insurance and HR contexts, subject to each organisation’s permissions and configuration. Benefit details and eligibility remain governed by the applicable policies and agreements.

  • Group medical insurance context
  • Organise company policies and documents
  • Follow requests and updates
DESIGNED AROUND REAL NEEDS

One experience,
different needs for every person.

iNex helps you build an organised understanding of your needs without assuming that individuals, families, employees or organisations are all the same. Start with what matters to you, then review the details with the appropriate provider.

01

Before choosing cover

Gather your questions, understand the terminology and identify the priorities you want to discuss when requesting a quote or consultation.

02

While managing a policy

Keep relevant information and documents organised, and pay attention to policy periods, renewals and changes that may call for a review.

03

When you need support

Use the service channels available in the app, and contact your broker or insurer for help interpreting terms or following a claim.
