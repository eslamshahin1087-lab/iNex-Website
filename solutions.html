PROTECTION STARTS WITH YOUR NEEDS

There is no single cover that fits everyone.

Individuals, families and businesses have different protection needs. Start by identifying what you want to protect, then review limits, exclusions and deductibles before comparing alternatives.

Compare protection fit—not price alone.

DESIGNED AROUND REAL NEEDS

One experience,
different needs for every person.

iNex helps you build an organised understanding of your needs without assuming that individuals, families, employees or organisations are all the same. Start with what matters to you, then review the details with the appropriate provider.

01

Before choosing cover

Gather your questions, understand the terminology and identify the priorities you want to discuss when requesting a quote or consultation.

02

While managing a policy

Keep relevant information and documents organised, and pay attention to policy periods, renewals and changes that may call for a review.

03

When you need support

Use the service channels available in the app, and contact your broker or insurer for help interpreting terms or following a claim.

MAKE EACH STEP COUNT

Make every step in your journey
clearer and more useful.

iNex helps you organise the questions and information you need before discussing options. The goal is not to rush into a product choice, but to build a clearer understanding of what may fit your needs and what should be verified.

01◎

Define what matters

Start with the people, property or responsibilities you want to protect, and consider how a potential risk could affect your life, family or work.

02▤

Review what you already have

Understand existing policies, benefits and limits. Do not assume that holding a policy means every need or circumstance is covered.

03⇄

Discuss options on a clear basis

Prepare your questions and ask the broker or insurer to explain differences in coverage, exclusions, procedures and terms before you decide.

Understand insurance. Protect what matters.
