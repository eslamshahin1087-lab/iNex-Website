ABOUT iNEX

We believe awareness
is part of protection.

iNex aims to make insurance easier to understand and the information and services around it easier to access. We want customers to move beyond simply holding a policy they may not fully understand, towards knowing what it means for them and what they should ask or review.

OUR PHILOSOPHY

Technology should make customers better informed and more confident to choose.

A better insurance experience starts with information people can understand, then moves to a realistic review of needs, exploration of options and service follow-up. We connect awareness with digital tools that help customers organise the journey without replacing the role of the broker or insurer.

That is why iNex brings together insurance awareness, protection assessment and customer services, while keeping the roles of the broker, insurer and policy terms clear.

Nature of service

iNex is an insurance awareness, information and digital-services platform connected to insurance brokerage services. It is not an insurer, does not underwrite risk and does not issue policies. Quote and consultation requests are handled through the licensed insurance broker; coverage, pricing and acceptance remain subject to the insurer’s terms and final policy.

STRATEGIC PARTNERSHIP

Two distinct platforms, a shared ambition for insurance.

iNex focuses on customer awareness and access to insurance services, while InsurNex provides an intelligent assistant for insurance brokers. Each platform serves its own audience and experience, with a strategic partnership supporting digital progress across insurance.

HOW WE BUILD

Clear principles guide
every iNex decision.

We measure technology by the value it creates for customers. That means starting with real questions, being transparent about service boundaries and designing each step to be clear and useful.

01

Clarity before complexity

We explain concepts in direct language, surface what needs attention and help customers distinguish general information from policy terms.

02

The customer at the centre

We start with the customer’s needs, questions and context—not a list of technical features. Every tool should lead to better understanding or a useful next step.

03

Responsibility and transparency

We make the roles of iNex, the broker and the insurer clear, and never promise coverage, acceptance or pricing beyond what the policy and provider determine.

WHAT GUIDES THE EXPERIENCE

We believe good technology
makes complex things clearer.

In insurance, a polished interface is not enough. Real value comes from information people can understand, steps they can follow and clear service boundaries. That is why the iNex experience is guided by practical principles that balance innovation with trust.

01◎

Knowledge before choice

We place awareness at the beginning of the journey so customers can understand key concepts and prepare questions before discussing cover or options.

02⌁

Connection instead of fragmentation

We connect educational content, needs-review tools, policy information and available services in a more organised digital journey.

03◇

Clarity of roles

We clarify what iNex provides and what belongs to the broker or insurer, while keeping the issued policy as the reference for interpreting cover.

04↗

Innovation that serves customers

We measure tools by how well they help customers understand, organise and reach a next step—not by the number of features or technical terms.
