THE iNEX PLATFORM

From knowledge to
more informed action.

iNex combines insurance education with digital services to help you move from a clear question to a practical next step: learn key concepts, review protection needs, organise policy information and requests, and use the support channels available in the app.

WHAT THE PLATFORM OFFERS

Tools to help you
understand your insurance.

The capabilities below are based on the current iNex application. Availability may vary by account type, configuration and insurance provider terms.

01 / AWARENESS
◎

Insurance tips & information

Explore insurance types, coverage, benefits, exclusions, deductibles, claims and renewals. Understand the language used in your policy, know where to look for key details and prepare the questions worth asking.

02 / ASSESSMENT
⌁

Protection assessment

Structure your view of personal or family protection needs and identify areas to review or discuss with an insurance broker.

03 / POLICIES
▤

Your policies & documents

View policies registered to your account, upload relevant documents and keep track of renewal dates. The policy issued by the insurer remains the authoritative record of coverage terms.

04 / QUOTES
⇄

Quote requests

Submit a quotation request, follow the options made available through the broker, and review differences in benefits, coverage limits, exclusions and terms before deciding.

05 / CUSTOMER CARE
♡

Consultations & customer care

Request a consultation or appointment, submit an enquiry or complaint through the channels available in the app, and contact the broker when you need clarification about a request or policy.

06 / REWARDS
✧

InsuraPoints

The programme provides a points and rewards mechanism linked to insurance, wellness and safe-driving activities, subject to programme rules and the applicable points criteria.

HOW THE EXPERIENCE WORKS

From information to service,
one step at a time.

You do not need to do everything at once. Use iNex at your own pace: learn first, review your needs, organise your information and then move to an available service or request guidance.

01

Learn

Start with content that explains insurance terms, types and policy components before you compare options.

02

Assess

Use protection assessment tools to review your needs and identify areas that require more information or discussion.

03

Organise

Bring policy information, documents and available requests together in your account, and keep track of dates and related steps.

04

Connect

When you need support, use the consultation and customer-service channels available, or discuss coverage terms with your broker or insurer.
