START HERE

Practical knowledge
starts with better questions.

You do not need to become an insurance expert to take a more informed next step. Start by understanding your policy, learn what to compare and prepare before you need to use your cover.

01

How to read your policy

Start with the policy parties, period of cover, sum insured and benefits schedule. Then review limits, exclusions, deductibles and notification and claims conditions.

02

How to compare insurance offers

Compare coverage type and limits, exclusions, provider networks and claims procedures on a like-for-like basis. Do not separate price from the benefits you receive.

03

What to do when making a claim

Check the notification process and deadlines in your policy. Keep correspondence, documents and reference numbers, and contact your broker or insurer when needed.
